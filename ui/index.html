ℹ About HAMU-GPT
What it is, how it works, and what it runs on
1

What this is

HAMU-GPT is a chat client for AI models. It has no model of its own, and it does not sit in the middle of your conversations.

You connect it to the AI providers you already use, and you talk to them directly through this window.

Your chats, your keys and your settings stay on your own machine.

2

How it works

  • Add a provider — paste its endpoint and your API key.
  • Press Detect — it identifies the provider and lists the models it offers.
  • Tick the models you want, and start chatting.

Nothing is routed through us — there is no server of ours holding your messages.

3

Your providers, your time, your credits

  • Providers. Every model you use belongs to a provider you added yourself. This app is only the window onto it.
  • Credits and quota. Any credit, free tier or rate limit is between you and that provider, on your own account. We never see it, and we cannot top it up for you.
  • Chat time. Time is counted only while a reply is actually being written — never while the window is simply sitting open. When it runs out you can refill it by watching a short ad, and your first sign-up comes with a one-time welcome gift.
4

Who made it

HAMU-GPT is made in Pakistan by SAMPHOR.

It was built as a single, self-contained app — no framework, and no third-party service holding your data. One person, a lot of patience, and a clear idea of what a chat client should feel like.
