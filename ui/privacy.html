Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This page explains what HAMU-GPT collects, where it is kept, who else sees it, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past. If something here is unclear, ask — the contact is at the bottom.

1. Who we are

HAMU-GPT is an independent chat client for AI models, made in Pakistan by SAMPHOR. We are not affiliated with any of the AI providers you connect, and we do not sell or resell access to any model.

2. What we collect

  • Your account. The email address you sign up with, your display name, and your password. The password is never stored as text — it is hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (200,000 rounds) and only the hash is kept.
  • Your chats. The messages you send and the replies you receive, so that your conversation history is there when you come back.
  • Your provider settings. The endpoints, API keys and model choices you add. These are stored so the app can talk to those providers on your behalf.
  • Basic technical data. Ordinary server logs (IP address, time, requested URL) that any web server produces, used only to keep the service running and to spot abuse.

We do not ask for your Google password, and we do not collect your contacts, your location, your files, or anything from your device.

3. Where your data is kept

This is the hosted website. Your account, your chats and your provider settings are stored on the server that runs this site — not on your own computer. The desktop version of HAMU-GPT keeps all of it on your own machine instead; if that matters to you, use the desktop app.

Provider API keys are encrypted at rest. The service runs on managed hosting, and the underlying host may keep short-lived copies of data as part of normal backups and operations.

4. Who else sees your data

  • The AI providers you add. When you send a message, it goes to the provider whose model you selected, using your own key. That provider's own privacy policy applies to it. We do not choose those providers and we do not see the contents of what you send beyond passing it through.
  • Our advertising partner. This site shows ads supplied by Adsterra. See the next section.
  • Our hosting provider. The infrastructure this site runs on.
  • Where the law requires it. If we are legally compelled to disclose something, we will — and we will tell you if we are allowed to.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not rent it. We do not trade it.

5. Cookies and advertising

This site displays advertising supplied by Adsterra, a third-party ad network. To do that, Adsterra and its partners may place and read cookies and similar identifiers in your browser. They use these to show ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to measure how ads perform.

When you first visit, we ask for your consent before any advertising code is loaded. If you decline, the advertising scripts are not loaded and the ad spaces stay empty. You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of the app.

We also store a small amount of your own preferences in your browser's local storage — your theme choice, your sidebar state, and your consent decision. That data never leaves your browser and is not used for advertising.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking them may mean ads become less relevant, but it will not stop you using the app.

6. How long we keep things

  • Your account and chats: until you delete them, or ask us to.
  • Server logs: a short period, for security and troubleshooting.
  • Advertising identifiers: controlled by Adsterra and your browser, not by us.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See your data. Your chats and settings are visible inside the app at any time.
  • Delete your data. You can remove individual chats from the sidebar, and you can ask us to delete your account and everything in it.
  • Withdraw consent for advertising cookies at any time, using the Cookie settings link.
  • Ask a question about any of this, using the contact below.

8. Children

This service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top will change and the change will be described here. Continuing to use the service after that means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions, data requests, or anything else on this page — write to gaju4678@gmail.com and we will answer.
